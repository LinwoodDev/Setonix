Setonix is a free, open source tabletop sandbox. Arrange cards, dice, chips, and boards, agree on your own rules, or choose a game mode that runs the rules for you. Play on your own, host friends, or join a dedicated server.

## Your first game

<Steps>

1. **Open Setonix.** [Download the app](/downloads/) or open the [web app](https://web.setonix.world). Local games do not need an account.
2. **Open Games.** Select a game mode to see its saved games, or choose **Sandbox** for unrestricted play. Use **Create** to start a new game.
3. **Choose a starting point.** The home screen’s **Create** action offers modes and saved templates under **Start**, and packs and a background under **Custom**. Creating from a selected mode goes straight to that mode’s setup. The core pack includes playing cards, dice, chips, chess pieces, and a chessboard.
4. **Name the game.** Give it a non-empty name and an optional description, then create it. Select a saved game to reopen it later.
5. **Set up the table.** Open the object library with the **+** button or **Tab**, then drag a deck, figure, or board onto a cell. Select a cell to see and move its contents.

</Steps>

## Pick how to play

| Starting point | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Custom sandbox | You arrange the pieces and follow your own rules. |
| Chess | A prepared chessboard and pieces; players enforce legal moves and turns themselves. |
| Blackjack | Scripted dealing, turns, scoring, and competitive or dealer rules. See the [Blackjack guide](/docs/v1/blackjack/). |
| Template | A copy of a table you previously prepared, ready for another session. |
| Multiplayer | A shared world hosted by another app or a dedicated server. |

Modes can restrict editing and provide their own toolbar, notes, and actions. If a control is unavailable, check the mode's rules and your server permissions.

## Find your way around

The game menu contains **Table**, **Waypoints**, **Zoom**, **Teams**, and **Settings**. Hosts also have **Advanced options** for packs and saving templates. The toolbar opens the library, chat, notes, and multiplayer controls; a scripted mode may replace sandbox controls with its own actions.

Local games save automatically as you play and when you leave. Export a game before moving it to another device. Browser saves belong to that browser's local storage; clearing site data can remove them.
