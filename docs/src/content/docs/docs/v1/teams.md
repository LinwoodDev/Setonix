---
title: Teams and visibility
description: Organize players and keep private hands visible to the right teams.
---

## Create teams and join them

Open **Game menu → Teams** to create or edit teams. Give each team a unique ID, an optional description, and a color. Use the team's membership controls to join or leave it.

A team can represent one player with a private hand, a group of cooperating players, or any other shared view of the table.

## Claim a cell

Open a cell's context menu and choose **Teams**. Select the teams that should have access to its contents. A cell with no teams selected is public. A claimed cell is not publicly visible; members of a team that claims it can see its contents.

For a private card hand, create a team for its player, join that team, and assign the hand's cell to it. Use separate teams and cells for the other players.

## Face-down objects

**Toggle hide** on a cell or object switches between a figure's face and back. Team claims control who can see private content; hiding a card changes how it is presented. Use both when a game calls for private hands and face-down piles.

The host processes visibility and sends filtered world updates to other clients. The host or dedicated server remains authoritative and has access to game state.

## Teams and roles

Teams organize play and visibility. Server roles control permissions such as editing the world, kicking players, or managing other users. Some game modes also keep their own game roles. See [multiplayer](/docs/v1/multiplayer/) and [server permissions](/docs/v1/server/permissions/) for those controls.
